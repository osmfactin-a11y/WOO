How it works

From blank wheel to winner in four steps

No account, no download, no waiting — the whole random name picker runs right here in your browser.

01

Add your options

Type names, tasks, or choices into the list. Add as many segments as you like.

02

03

See the winner instantly, share the link, or remove it and spin again.
