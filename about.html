About us

A wheel, built to just work

WooWheel started from a simple annoyance: most spin-wheel sites are slow, covered in pop-ups, or ask you to sign up before you can even add your first name.

What we're building

We wanted one thing that loads fast, works the same on a phone in a classroom as it does on a laptop during a livestream, and never gets in the way of the actual decision you're trying to make. No account, no watermark, no forced waiting period before your prize wheel spins.

WooWheel is a small, focused tool rather than a bloated "all-in-one" app. It does the spin-wheel job — random name picker, decision maker, raffle drawer, party game — and tries to do it well.

What we care about

  • Speed first. The wheel should spin the moment you tap it, not after a loading screen.
  • Fair by default. Every option gets an equal, genuinely random chance unless you choose otherwise.
  • Your data stays yours. Wheels and saved presets live in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • No clutter. One page, one job, and content that's actually useful if you want to read it.

Who uses WooWheel

Teachers picking a student fairly, streamers running a live giveaway, teams deciding who takes the next task, families arguing over dinner plans, and plenty of people who just wanted an excuse to watch something spin. If you've got a decision and a short list of options, WooWheel is built for you.

Questions or feedback?

We're actively improving WooWheel. If something breaks, feels slow, or you have an idea for a feature, we'd like to hear about it — visit our contact page to get in touch.
