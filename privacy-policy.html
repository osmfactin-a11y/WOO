Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

This policy explains what information WooWheel ("we", "us", "the site") collects when you use woowheel.com, and how it's used. We built WooWheel to run almost entirely in your own browser, so most of what you type into it never reaches a server at all.

1. Information stored in your browser

The options you type into the wheel, your saved wheel presets, and settings like theme, sound, and spin speed are stored using your browser's local storage. This data:

  • Stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers
  • Is not shared with us or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data

The only exception is if you deliberately use the "Share link" or "Embed" feature — in that case, your wheel's options are encoded directly into the URL you choose to share, not stored on a server.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) for two purposes: remembering your preferences, and — where enabled — supporting analytics and advertising, described below.

3. Advertising and third-party services

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other advertising partners. These partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can also visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of many third-party ad networks at once.

We may also use privacy-friendly analytics tools to understand overall traffic (for example, how many people visit, which pages are popular) without identifying individual visitors personally.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for or store your name, email, or account details to use the spin wheel tool itself
  • We don't sell personal data
  • We don't read or transmit the contents of your wheel unless you actively choose to generate a share link

5. Children's privacy

WooWheel is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our contact form, please reach out via our contact page so we can address it.

6. Your choices

You can clear your saved wheels and preferences at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, or by using the "Reset wheel" button in the tool. You can also opt out of personalised advertising using the links in section 3.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site or applicable regulations change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, reach us through our contact page.

This page is a general privacy notice template and isn't a substitute for legal advice. If you plan to run ads or collect user data at scale, consider having a lawyer review this policy for your specific situation and jurisdiction.
