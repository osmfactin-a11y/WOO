When a yes/no wheel helps

Not every decision deserves a pros-and-cons list. For low-stakes calls — order dessert or not, text them back tonight or tomorrow, take the meeting or skip it — a quick spin removes the overthinking.

Make it your own

Open the full wheel builder to rename the two options (Maybe/Definitely, True/False, Left/Right), adjust the odds if you want one answer more likely, or add a third option.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as flipping a coin?

It works the same way — a fair 50/50 random pick — just presented as a spinning wheel instead of a coin flip.

Can I weight it so yes is more likely?

Yes, open the full wheel builder, turn on custom odds, and set a higher weight on "Yes".
