Built for live draws

The spinning motion, tick sound, and confetti finish make the raffle wheel genuinely watchable — useful for livestream giveaways, community raffles, and in-person events where the audience wants to see the moment of the draw, not just a name flashed on screen.

Good to know before running a paid raffle

WooWheel is a general-purpose random picker and isn't certified for regulated gambling or legally binding prize draws. If your raffle involves paid entries or a legal obligation to award a prize, confirm compliance with your local laws separately. See our Terms of Service for details.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for a paid giveaway?

You can use the wheel to pick the winner, but check your local laws first — see our Terms of Service for the details.

Can I remove winners so they can't win twice?

Yes, enable "remove after spin" in the full wheel builder so each ticket can only win once.

Can I share the exact raffle wheel with my audience?

Yes, use the "Share link" button in the full builder to generate a link with your entries already loaded.
